**Self-hosted Cerb is free to run.** Community installations have the [documentation](/docs/home/), the [guides](/guides/), the video channel, and the discussion forums to draw on, and we answer email as we're able – no response time is promised, but we'd rather help than leave anyone stuck.

Direct support with a same-business-day response is included with every subscription, self-hosted and Cerb Cloud alike.

## Standard Support

Included with every subscription

- Support by email
- Same business day responses
- Concept-level guidance
- Covers the latest major version (12.x)

Cerb Cloud

- Mandatory major version upgrades within one week of notice

## Enterprise Support

$500 per month

No long-term commitment

- Support by email or Slack (voice and screen sharing)
- Private Slack channel for up to three team members
- Same business day responses
- Emergency support within 1 hour (nights, weekends, holidays)
- Hands-on guidance for your specific implementation
- Developer support for automations, API, integrations, and plugins
- Covers the last two major versions (11.x and 12.x)

Cerb Cloud

- Schedule major software updates at your convenience
- Early access to beta versions
- Staging environment to test new features before upgrading
- Install custom plugins
