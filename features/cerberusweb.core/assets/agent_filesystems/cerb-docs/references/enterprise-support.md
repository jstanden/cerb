## Standard Support

Included with every license

- Support by email and forums
- Same business day responses
- Concept-level guidance
- Covers the latest major version (11.x)

Cerb Cloud

- Mandatory major version upgrades within one week of notice

## Enterprise Support

$500 per month

No long-term commitment

- Support by email or Slack (voice and screen sharing)
- Private Slack channel for up to three team members
- Same business day responses
- Emergency support within 1 hour (nights, weekends, holidays)
- Hands-on guidance for your specific implementation
- Developer support for automations, API, integrations, and plugins
- Covers the last two major versions (10.x and 11.x)

Cerb Cloud

- Schedule major software updates at your convenience
- Early access to beta versions
- Staging environment to test new features before upgrading
- Install custom plugins
